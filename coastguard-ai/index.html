The Problem

The Challenge

Gujarat's 1,600+ km coastline is one of India's most cyclone-prone regions. The Arabian Sea generates powerful cyclones that strike with little warning, threatening millions of coastal residents, thousands of fishermen at sea, and critical infrastructure.

Traditional disaster response systems struggle with data fragmentation, slow communication chains, and lack of hyperlocal risk intelligence — costing precious hours when every minute matters.

CoastGuard AI demonstrates how an Agentic AI architecture could transform coastal disaster preparedness through coordinated, intelligent, real-time decision support.

Key Challenges
⏱

Delayed Warnings

Critical hours lost between cyclone detection and ground-level alerts.

📍

Hyperlocal Gaps

District-level warnings miss village-level and coastal micro-risks.

🚢

Fishermen at Sea

Thousands of fishing vessels tracked individually during emergencies.

🏃

Evacuation Chaos
