Cyclone Preparedness

What To Do — Before, During & After

⚡

Before Cyclone

Secure all important documents in waterproof container
Charge all mobile devices and power banks
Store 3+ days of drinking water and food
Follow official weather warnings from IMD
Prepare a family emergency kit (medicines, torch)
Know your nearest evacuation shelter location
Secure loose objects around your home
Share your plan with family members
🌀

During Cyclone

Stay indoors away from windows and doors
Avoid all coastal, river, and low-lying areas
Follow official evacuation orders immediately
Do not travel unnecessarily on roads
Listen to NDMA / State DMA radio broadcasts
Do not use electrical equipment during storm
Keep emergency contacts readily available
If evacuating, follow designated routes only
🏗

After Cyclone

Avoid entering damaged buildings or structures
Report all emergencies to official helplines
Follow official instructions before returning home
Assist elderly, disabled and vulnerable persons
Do not touch downed power lines or cables
Boil water before consumption until cleared
Document property damage for insurance claims
Register at relief camps if home is uninhabitable
⚠ DEMO CHECKLIST — In a real emergency always follow official government advisories from IMD, NDMA, and your State DMA.
